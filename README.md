# Hostel Finder

A website where students can find registered hostels in Ghana, and where hostels pay their subscription and taxes.

## What it does
- Lists registered hostels with name, owner contact, location, landmark, price, photos and videos
- Hostels subscribe before they are listed, and every payment is recorded
- Landlords can remove a room from the list once it is taken
- Later: monthly tax schedule with automatic payment split

## Who uses it
- Students: search and view hostels
- Landlords: add hostels and manage rooms
- Admin (me): approve hostels and track payments

## Build order
1. Hostel listing pages
2. Landlord login
3. Subscription and payment records
4. Tax schedule and automatic split
